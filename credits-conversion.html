Credits conversion

Celonis consumption across AI, Compute, Storage, Data Transfer, and Other Services is unified into a single Credits balance. Each lane meters usage in its own unit (tokens, CPU-hours, terabytes) and has its own Credit rate, which can vary further by sub-type. Expand a lane below to see its rates.
AI Tokens

AI token usage is metered separately for input and output tokens, each with its own Credit rate per 10 million tokens.

Token typeCredits
Input0.2 Credits / 10M tokens
Output0.1 Credits / 10M tokens
Compute

Compute usage is metered per CPU-hour, with a different Credit rate depending on the workload type.

Workload typeCredits
ETL0.15 Credits / CPU-hr
QE0.3 Credits / CPU-hr
DI0.4 Credits / CPU-hr
Storage

Storage usage is metered per 10 TB, with a different Credit rate depending on the storage engine.

Storage typeCredits
Iceberg0.1 Credits / 10 TB
S30.15 Credits / 10 TB
Data Transfer

Data Transfer (egress) usage is metered per 10 TB at a single Credit rate.

Credits
0.15 Credits / 10 TB
Other Services

Rate not yet defined for this lane.
